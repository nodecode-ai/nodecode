Nodecode itself is free, under the MIT license, and runs on your own provider key. The hosted models at [nodecode.ai](https://nodecode.ai/pricing) are paid per token from a balance you top up, as on DeepSeek's platform. Plans are not sold now.

{/* source: web/src/lib/topup.ts, web/src/lib/topup-rules.ts, web/src/lib/lab-plans.ts (`TOPUP_MIN`, `TOPUP_MAX`) */}
## Topping up

Top up on [Top up](https://nodecode.ai/account/top-up), in euros or dollars, a whole amount from 5 to 10,000 at a time. The amount is before tax: a top-up in euros adds 21% Dutch VAT on top, one in dollars none. The balance gets the amount you chose.

A new account tops up at most 100 at a time and 200 a day, until its first top-up is 14 days old. Any account tops up at most 10,000 a day and opens at most five checkouts an hour.

Then sign in with `nodecode login --api`: see [A nodecode.ai account](first-run.mdx).

## What a call costs

A call costs its tokens at the model's price: input the model had cached, the rest of the input, and the output, each per million tokens; an image model by the image. The prices are the same number in euros or dollars, before tax, and are on [the pricing page](https://nodecode.ai/pricing#api).

| Model | Cached input | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cyber v1 | 0.006 | 0.30 | 1.20 |
| Decide v1 | 0.10 | 0.10 | Free |
| Image v1 | 0.04 an image | | |

Cyber v1 costs DeepSeek's own price for the model it is built on, at every hour. Its context is 1M tokens; a call with more than 256K tokens of input pays four times the cached price for its cached input. A typical call of our own agent, 89K cached and 2.5K new input tokens and 1.3K output, costs about 0.0028 on Cyber v1, so a top-up of 5 pays for about 1,750 of them.

The cost is taken once the model has answered and shows beside the call under [Usage](https://nodecode.ai/account/usage). The tokens are the model's own count; when the model reports none, they are estimated from the size of the call and its answer, about four bytes a token. A balance held in both currencies is spent in euros first.

Costs nothing:

- Listing the models (`/v1/models`).
- Asking what is left (`/v1/usage`): the balance and the requests running now, which `nodecode` prints after `nodecode login` and at `/usage`.
- A call refused because your requests at once are all busy.
- A call the model itself refused or failed, such as a prompt past its window.

## Requests at once

An account runs 4 requests at the same time across all your machines and keys. One more waits for a free place, up to 30 seconds, and is then answered with `429`. A refused call costs nothing.

{/* source: crates/gate/src/error.rs (`insufficient_balance`) */}
## When the balance is used up

A call is answered with `402` and `insufficient_balance`, saying where to top up. Top up and the next call goes through; nothing else needs to change.
