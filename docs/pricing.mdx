## Topping up

Top up on [Top up](https://nodecode.ai/account/top-up), in euros or dollars, a whole amount from 2 to 20,000 at a time. The balance gets the amount you chose. Once your top-ups come to 2 or more, the account can also make a [free key](models.mdx#free-key), which runs Cyber v1 from the Nodecode CLI at no cost.

The amount is before tax. 21% Dutch VAT goes on top when you pay in euros, or when you top up from the European Union in either currency. A business gives its name, and in the EU its VAT number, which the EU's register (VIES) confirms before the checkout opens: a Dutch number pays Dutch VAT on an invoice that names the business, another EU country's number pays none (reverse charge).

A new account tops up at most 1,000 at a time and 2,000 a day, until its first top-up is 14 days old. Any account tops up at most 20,000 a day and opens at most 5 checkouts an hour. Once one card has been declined 3 times in an hour, no new checkout opens until that hour is over.

Then sign in with `nodecode login --api`: see [A nodecode.ai account](first-run.mdx).

## Auto top-up

Auto top-up refills the balance from a saved card, so a long job is not cut off by an empty balance. It is off until you turn it on under [Top up](https://nodecode.ai/account/top-up), pick three figures and accept its terms:

- **When:** the balance, euros and dollars together, falls below 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100.
- **How much:** any amount a top-up may be, within the limits above.
- **Monthly limit:** required, up to 100,000. A charge never takes the calendar month, on Amsterdam's clock, past it.

The card is saved through a checkout that charges nothing. Each charge comes at least 5 minutes after the one before, carries VAT as a top-up does, and is mailed to you as an invoice. The first declined charge turns auto top-up off and mails you why; it stays off until you turn it on again.

## What a call costs

A call costs its tokens at the model's price: input the model had cached, the rest of the input, and the output, each per million tokens; an image model by the image. The prices are the same number in euros or dollars, before tax, and are on [the pricing page](https://nodecode.ai/pricing#api).

| Model | Cached input | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cyber v1 | 0.009 | 0.33 | 1.32 |
| Decide v1 | 0.04 | 0.04 | Free |
| Image v1 | 0.04 an image | | |

These are the weekday prices, the same at every hour. Every weekend, from Friday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 Amsterdam time, every token costs 50% less: Cyber v1 0.0045, 0.165 and 0.66, Decide v1's input 0.02. An image keeps its price. A call is priced by when it starts.

Cyber v1's context window is 1,048,576 tokens (1M); a longer prompt is refused by the model and costs nothing. A request with more than 262,144 tokens of input pays four times the cached input price for its cached input. A typical call of our own agent, 89K cached and 2.5K new input tokens and 1.3K output, costs about 0.0033 on Cyber v1 on a weekday, so a top-up of 2 pays for about 600 of them, and about 1,200 at the weekend.

The cost is taken once the model has answered and shows beside the call under [Usage](https://nodecode.ai/account/usage). The tokens are the model's own count; when the model reports none, they are estimated from the size of the call and its answer, about four bytes a token. A balance held in both currencies is spent in euros first.

Costs nothing:

- A call on a [free key](models.mdx#free-key).
- Listing the models (`/v1/models`).
- Asking what is left (`/v1/usage`): the balance and the requests running now, which `nodecode` prints after `nodecode login` and at `/usage`.
- A call refused because your requests at once are all busy.
- A call the model itself refused or failed, such as a prompt past its window.

## Requests at once

An account runs 12 requests at the same time across all your machines and keys. One more waits for a free place, up to 30 seconds, and is then answered with `429`. A refused call costs nothing.

{/* source: crates/gate/src/error.rs (`insufficient_balance`) */}
## When the balance is used up

A call is answered with `402` and `insufficient_balance`, saying where to top up. Top up and the next call goes through; nothing else needs to change. With [auto top-up](#auto-top-up) on, the balance is refilled once it falls below the figure you picked, while the monthly limit allows.
